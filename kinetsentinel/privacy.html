KinetSentinel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

KinetSentinel is a local permission auditor for AI Agents on macOS. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

KinetSentinel does not collect, transmit, store (off-device), or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All monitoring, rules, and alerts live entirely on your Mac.

Data Storage

Your rules and audit history are stored locally on your device. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

KinetSentinel uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

KinetSentinel contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
