FAQ

Why does Sentinel need Accessibility permission? / 为什么需要辅助功能权限?

Sentinel monitors TCC permission changes and agent process activity, which requires the Accessibility and Automation grants. Sentinel only reads permission state — it never grants permissions on your behalf.

What counts as a "sensitive action"? / 什么算敏感动作?

Screen capture, keystroke-level access, automation of other apps (Apple Events), and reads of protected folders by agent processes. You define the watch rules.

Does Sentinel phone home? / 会联网上报吗?

No. All monitoring and alerting is local. No account, no telemetry, no network. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里?

Entirely on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes all data.
