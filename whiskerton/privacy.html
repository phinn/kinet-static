Pawsbury

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Pawsbury collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind. Your town — houses, pets, coins, decorations — lives only on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your save file (houses, pets, items, coins) is a small file inside the app's own storage.
  • Your settings and preferences stay in local app storage.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy, because there is no server.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.
  • We do not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not ask for an account, email, or any permission.

Purchases

Pawsbury offers an optional one-time purchase and optional auto-renewable subscriptions ("Whisker Pass"). All payment handling is done by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details, and purchase information is managed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Pawsbury is rated 4+ (and equivalents in other stores). There is no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and nothing is collected from anyone of any age.
