Support & FAQ

A cozy pet town for folding and non-folding iPhones alike

Still stuck? Email phinn@outlook.com — a human answers, usually within two business days.

How do my pets make items?

Every pet has its own specialty (Mochi makes yarn balls, Biscuit bakes bone cookies…). A ring in each room fills as your pet works. When it is full, tap Collect — the item goes to your Inventory. Production continues while the app is closed; your town greets you with everything that finished while you were away.

Why can't I adopt another pet?

Each pet needs its own room. When every room is occupied, buy a bigger house — larger houses come with more rooms. The Street screen lists all six houses and what they cost.

What do coins buy?

Coins buy new houses and room decorations. Decorations speed up production in the room where you place them. Selling items from the Inventory earns coins; with an active Whisker Pass, every sale earns 25% more.

What does the legendary lock mean?

Legendary pets (Starwhisk, Moonbun, Emberling) are part of the optional Whisker Pass. While the Pass is active you can adopt them for free. If your Pass ends later, the legendary pets you already adopted stay in your town and keep producing forever.

Whisker Pass — lifetime, monthly, yearly

Lifetime is a one-time purchase that never expires. Monthly and Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions charged to your Apple ID. They renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Restore purchases

Got a new device or reinstalled the game? Open the Whisker Pass screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your Pass is tied to your Apple ID and comes back without paying again.

Resetting my town

Settings → Reset Town starts a fresh town. It never affects purchases — your Whisker Pass is stored with Apple, not in the save file.

Privacy

The game has no network connection at all and collects nothing. Full policy here.
