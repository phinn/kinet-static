Novette Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Overview

Novette is an offline cosmic merge game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

Novette does not collect, transmit, store (off-device), or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no sign-in.

Data Storage

Your best scores, daily streaks and preferences are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

Novette uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Novette contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.

Contact
