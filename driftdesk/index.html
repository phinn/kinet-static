FAQ

How do I start a soundscape? / 怎么播放声景?

Click the DriftDesk icon in the menu bar and pick a scene. Click the icon again or press Esc to stop.

Can I mix sounds? / 可以混音吗?

Each scene is a complete, carefully balanced mix. Volume is adjustable from the panel slider.

Does it use the network? / 会联网吗?

Never. All sound is generated on your Mac in real time. No streaming, no account, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里?

Entirely on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes all data.
