Zero collection

Pagebench — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026. The short version: Pagebench collects nothing.

Pagebench has no network access. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Your PDFs and your handwritten notes are stored only on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Documents you import are copied into the app's own folder on your device (Files → On My iPhone → Pagebench).
  • Your handwritten notes are saved on-device, tied to each document.
  • Preferences (ink settings, paper style, flip mode) are stored in the app's local settings.

Deleting a document inside Pagebench deletes its file and notes from the device. Nothing is backed up by us, because there is no "us" on the network path.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not require an account or an email address.
  • We do not read the contents of your documents for any purpose other than displaying them to you.

Children

Pagebench is rated 4+ and contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no advertising. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date. Changes will never introduce data collection retroactively.
