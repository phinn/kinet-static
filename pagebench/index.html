What it does

Paired reading & writing

Import any PDF. The document page sits on one pane; your ink sits on the other, bound to that page. Flip the page and the right note page comes with it.

Real ink, palm-safe

Apple Pencil pressure, four inks, three widths, a real eraser, undo & redo. By default only the Pencil draws — resting your palm never leaves a mark.

Unlink on purpose, relink in one tap

Want to compare two pages? Flip separately. A badge shows when a note is unlinked, and one button brings you back to its page.

Export the pair

Share one image with the document page and your notes side by side. No watermark, full resolution.
