Inkmist Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Inkmist (墨霭) is an offline ink-painting puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

Inkmist does not collect, transmit, store (off-device), or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Data Storage

Your level progress and best scores are stored locally on your device only. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

Inkmist uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Inkmist contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.
