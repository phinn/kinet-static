Privacy

CubeLike — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the CubeLike iOS app.

Short version: CubeLike collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network access. Your progress lives on your device and never leaves it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Progress — your unlocked levels and star ratings, stored locally on your device.
  • Current board — the level you have in progress, so you can put the game down and pick it up later.
  • Settings — your language choice, stored locally.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of the above permanently. Nothing is backed up to us because there is no "us" to back it up to.

What the app does not do

  • No data collection of any kind — the App Store privacy label lists none, because there is none.
  • No third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no ads, no tracking.
  • No network requests at runtime. The game is fully offline; it works in airplane mode.
  • No third-party content — all art, sound and text are original.

Children

CubeLike contains no violence, no mature content and no chat of any kind. Because it collects no data, no data of children is processed either.
