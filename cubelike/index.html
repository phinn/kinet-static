A take-turns puzzle wrapped around a cube. Tap a tile to charge its arrows; at three it bursts and fires across the faces — one good tap can sweep the whole cube.

How it plays

  • Tap to charge. Every tap adds one arrow to a tile. Three arrows and it bursts.
  • Bursts chain. A burst shoots four ways, charging neighbors — a cascade that crosses edges and wraps around all six faces.
  • Depth pays. Your score is 10 points × chain depth, so one deep cascade beats a dozen shallow taps.
  • Cascade taps are free. Tiles lit up by a chain don't spend your moves — only the tap you make does.
