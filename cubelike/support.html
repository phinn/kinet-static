CubeLike — Help

Questions about the cube, the cascade, or your stars — start here. Most answers are one tap away.

Playing basics

  • How do I play? Tap any tile with 1–2 arrows. Each tap adds one arrow; at three arrows the tile bursts and fires in four directions, charging its neighbors.
  • What is a chain? When a burst charges a neighbor to three, that neighbor bursts too — and so on. Each link in the cascade multiplies your score (10 × depth).
  • What do the board colors mean? Dark blocked tiles are solid: they never burst and never count. Everything else is fair game, on every face of the cube.
  • How do I win a level? Reach the target score before your moves run out. Finishing with 3+ moves left earns 3 stars; with 1–2 left, 2 stars; otherwise 1.
  • Why did my moves not go down? Tiles charged by a cascade are free — only the tile you tap yourself spends a move.

Frequently asked

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only — levels, stars and the board in progress. No account, no cloud, no sync. Uninstalling clears everything.
  • Is the game online? No. CubeLike makes no network requests at all — no ads, no analytics, nothing to sign into.
  • How do levels work? Every level is dealt from a fixed seed: same number, same board. Moves shrink and targets rise as you climb, and more blocked tiles join the board.
  • Can I replay earlier levels? Yes — the level grid stays open for everything you have unlocked, and you can chase a better star rating any time.
  • Which languages are supported? English, 简体中文, 繁體中文 and 日本語 — CubeLike follows your device's language automatically.
  • Does it run on iPad? CubeLike is designed for iPhone and plays great one-handed.

Troubleshooting

  • A tile won't tap. Empty tiles (no arrows) can't be charged directly — start from a tile that already shows 1 or 2 arrows, or burst a neighbor into it.
  • I ran out of moves. Retry the level and look for a burst point beside two charged tiles — deep chains beat scattered taps every time.
  • Sound is off? Check the mute switch and volume. CubeLike's sounds follow your device's silent state.
