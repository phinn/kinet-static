Cambrer — 弯音折叠

A string instrument you play with the hinge. Half-open for a fifth, flat for a minor third — the fold is your bow.

Playing basics

  • Hold the fold angle to set the pitch interval — the app maps hinge position to string tension.
  • Tap or glide on the strings with your other hand to pluck.
  • Pick a scale in Settings to stay melodic while you explore.
  • Record a loop, then layer a second pass over it.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound? Flip the mute switch, raise volume, and check Bluetooth headphones aren't stealing output.
  • Pitch jumps oddly? Re-calibrate in Settings → Hinge. Fully close the phone once so the sensor can re-zero.
  • Recording won't save? Microphone access is only used for the loop layer; enable it in iOS Settings if you declined on first launch.
