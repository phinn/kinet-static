FAQ

Which formats are supported? / 支持哪些格式?

PDF, PNG, JPEG and HEIC images. Text is extracted with on-device OCR — including handwriting for common cases.

Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里?

Entirely on your Mac. Index, OCR text and original files stay local. No account, no cloud, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I search inside scanned PDFs? / 扫描版 PDF 能搜吗?

Yes — that's exactly what on-device OCR is for. Import once, search forever, fully offline.

Will I lose my library if I delete the app? / 删除应用会丢资料库吗?

The library is a folder of your files plus an index. Deleting the app keeps your files; re-import to rebuild the index.
