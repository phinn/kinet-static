FAQ

How many pets can I track? / 可以记录几只宠物?

Unlimited. Each pet gets its own profile with its own visit history, weight chart and vaccine timeline.

Can I export my data? / 能导出数据吗?

Yes — export any pet's full record for vets or backup. Your data is yours.

Does FurChart sync to the cloud? / 会同步到云端吗?

No. Everything is stored locally on your Mac, private by design. No account, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app? / 删除应用会丢数据吗?

Export first. Deleting the app deletes its local database. Use the export function for backup before uninstalling.
