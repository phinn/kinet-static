Shotframe 隐私政策

Shotframe · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 · English below

一句话版本

Shotframe 不联网，也不收集任何数据。没有服务器、没有账号、没有广告、没有统计 SDK、没有第三方追踪。

它做什么

Shotframe 把你自己导入的 App 截图，按 App Store 要求的像素尺寸套上标题、配色和画框，导出成可以直接上传的首图。全部处理都在你的设备上完成。

你的数据

  • 导入的截图：复制到 App 的本地存储里，只用于生成首图，不上传。
  • 标题、配色、尺寸选择：保存在本机。
  • 导出的图片：写进你的相册或通过系统分享面板交给你，App 本身不留副本。
  • 开发者无法访问上述任何数据。卸载 App 即全部删除。

网络

Shotframe 不发起任何网络请求。App 没有联网逻辑，也没有崩溃上报、统计或广告 SDK。

权限说明

  • 相册读取：仅在你主动选择截图时读取你选中的那几张。
  • 相册写入：仅在你点「存到相册」时写入生成的首图。
  • App 不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、健康等任何其他权限。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其任何信息。

变更

若本政策发生实质变更，将随 App 更新在本页公布。

Privacy Policy

Shotframe · Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Shotframe makes no network requests and collects no data. No server, no account, no ads, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking.

What it does

Shotframe takes the app screenshots you import, wraps them in a headline, palette and frame at the exact pixel size the App Store requires, and exports store-ready images. All processing happens on your device.

Your data

  • Imported screenshots are copied into local app storage and used only to render posters. They are never uploaded.
  • Headlines, palette and size choices are stored locally.
  • Exported images go to your photo library or to the system share sheet. The app keeps no copy.
  • The developer has no access to any of it. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Network

Shotframe makes no network requests of any kind. There is no networking code, no crash reporting, no analytics and no ad SDK.

Permissions

  • Photo library read: only the images you explicitly pick.
  • Photo library write: only when you tap Save to Photos.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or health access is requested.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an app update.
