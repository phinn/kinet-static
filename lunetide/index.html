Lunetide

The moon, quietly in your menu bar.

Lunetide tracks tonight's moon: moonrise and moonset to the minute, the current phase drawn live, illumination and lunar age at a glance. Real astronomy computed on your Mac — never an estimate, never a network call.

View on the Mac App Store →

What it tells you

Moonrise & set

Exact times for your city, today and tomorrow.

Live phase

Waxing, waning, quarter or full — drawn as it is right now.

Illumination

What percent of the disc is lit, to the digit.

Lunar age

Days since new moon, plus the next full & new moon dates.

Real astronomy, not lookup tables

Lunetide solves the moon's position directly — ELP-style truncated series for lunar longitude, latitude and parallax, then rises and sets from the moon's altitude at your coordinates. Works for any city, any date, any century you'll live in. Polar cases where the moon never rises or never sets are handled honestly.

Made for the times you look up

Photographers planning a moonlit shoot, anglers watching tides, stargazers waiting for a dark new-moon night, or anyone who just wants to know why the sky is bright tonight. The menu bar shows a live phase glyph; the panel shows tonight's full story.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account. No network. No analytics. Location is a city you pick from a list, not a sensor. Everything is computed locally inside the app's sandbox.
