Frequently asked questions, answered by a human.

Contact

Write to phinn@outlook.com — include the word "Paravent" in the subject and I'll reply within a day or two.

Do I need a foldable phone?

No. On a regular iPhone, Paravent runs as a flat workbench: pick a member on the left, adjust hit points and conditions on the right. The tent (two-face) mode is for foldables with a hinge.

What do my players see?

Only what you push over: the scene card, the public initiative order and the mood timer. Your notes, their exact hit points and monster numbers never appear on the player face — that separation is the whole point of a screen.

Which rules set does the bestiary use?

The app ships with a 30-creature subset of the SRD 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0). The full attribution is shown inside the app under Settings → SRD 5.1 attribution.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. There is no account, no sync, no network connection. Deleting the app deletes your campaigns.
