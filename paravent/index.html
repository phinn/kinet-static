The screen between you and the table

Tent your foldable open like a desk calendar: the top screen faces the players — a scene card, the initiative order and a mood timer, nothing else. The bottom screen faces you: initiative, hit points, conditions and a dice tray that reads expressions like 2d6+3.

On a regular iPhone, Paravent runs as a flat workbench — members on the left, details on the right. On iPad, the whole war room spreads out side by side.

What's inside

  • One-tap initiative for the whole table — roll all, delay, advance, push to the player face
  • Hit points with ±1 / ±5 / ±10 quick steps, 14 conditions and concentration saves
  • A dice tray that parses — advantage, disadvantage, and a running history
  • Scene cards with twelve handcrafted moods, pushed to the players in one tap
  • A bestiary of 30 creatures (SRD 5.1 subset, CC-BY-4.0) — searchable, offline
  • Session clock and a closed-cover display that keeps the time when the lid shuts
