Privacy Policy

Short version: nothing leaves your device.

Data collection

Paravent collects no data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network permission. It does not create an account and does not know who you are.

Data storage

Your campaigns, encounters, notes and settings are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of this data permanently.

Third parties

None. Paravent embeds a subset of the SRD 5.1 rules document (CC-BY-4.0) as reference data; it contains no personal information and makes no network requests.

Children

Paravent is a tabletop gaming tool and collects no data from anyone, of any age.
