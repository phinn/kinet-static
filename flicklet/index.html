Swipe to a clean photo library

What is Flicklet?

Flicklet turns photo cleanup into a fast, satisfying swipe: left to delete, right to keep. It groups similar shots, screenshots and bursts so you can review them in seconds — entirely on your device.

Privacy

  • No account, no tracking, no ads. Flicklet contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Photos never leave your device. All scanning, clustering and processing happens locally using Apple's Photos and Vision frameworks.
  • No data collected. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is nothing to encrypt in transit because nothing is sent.
  • Deletions require your confirmation through the system photo-library permission dialog. Flicklet only moves photos to the Recently Deleted album, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Permissions

Flicklet requests access to your photo library solely to display and manage your own photos. You can revoke access anytime in Settings → Privacy → Photos.
