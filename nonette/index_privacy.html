Privacy Policy

Nonette · Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Nonette collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. All data stays on your device.

Data we access

  • Progress & streaks — stored locally on your device only.
  • Daily puzzle state — generated from the date on your device; no server involved.

Third parties

Nonette uses no third-party SDKs, no ad networks, and no analytics services. The app does not make network requests.

Children

Nonette contains no objectionable content and collects no data, so it is safe for all ages.

Contact

Questions? Reach us via the support link on the App Store page.
