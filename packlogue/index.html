What is Packlogue?

Packlogue is a packing log. Start trips from reusable templates, check items off as you pack, then run a two-minute debrief when you get home: what was just right, what ran out, what stayed unused. Your learnings are written back to the template, so your fifth trip packs smarter than any generic checklist.

Privacy

  • No account, no tracking, no ads. Packlogue contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No data collected. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything stays on your device.
  • No network access. Packlogue works fully offline; there is nothing to send anywhere.
  • Your data, your control. Templates and trips are stored locally. Deleting the app deletes everything — nothing is kept on our side.

Permissions

Packlogue requests no special permissions. No photos, no location, no contacts — just your packing lists.
