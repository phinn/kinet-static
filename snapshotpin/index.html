Snapshot Pin

Screenshot. Annotate. Pin. · 截图标注,钉在桌面随手看 · 撮って、注釈、ピン留め。

Quick Start

⇧⌘9 — Capture a region → annotate → pin it to your desktop.
⇧⌘0 — Show / hide all pins.

zh-Hans: ⇧⌘9 框选屏幕任意区域,标注后钉在桌面;⇧⌘0 显示/隐藏全部钉图。
zh-Hant: ⇧⌘9 框選螢幕任意範圍,標記後釘在桌面;⇧⌘0 顯示/隱藏全部釘圖。
日本語: ⇧⌘9 範囲を選択して注釈、デスクトップにピン留め;⇧⌘0 すべてのピンを表示/非表示。

Working with Pins

• Drag a pin anywhere to move it · 拖动即可移动钉图
• Scroll on a pin to zoom · 滚轮缩放
• Hover the pin's top edge for the toolbar: copy, save as PNG, opacity, close
• Double-click a pin to close it · 双击关闭

Screen Recording Permission

Snapshot Pin requires Screen Recording permission to capture regions of your screen. The captured image is processed entirely on your Mac — nothing is ever uploaded. You can revoke this permission anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording.
