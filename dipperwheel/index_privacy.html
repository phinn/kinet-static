Privacy Policy

Dipperwheel 斗转 · Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Dipperwheel collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. All computation happens on your device.

Data we access

  • Date & time — read from your device clock to compute solar terms and pentads. Never leaves the device.
  • Preferences — language and appearance settings, stored locally.

Third parties

Dipperwheel uses no third-party SDKs, no ad networks, and no analytics services. The app does not make network requests.

Children

Dipperwheel is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
