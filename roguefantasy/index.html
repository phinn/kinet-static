RogueFantasy

A turn-based JRPG roguelike in a living 3D world
3 heroes · 4 acts · day-night cycle · weather · chocobos & airships

The Mist takes one person every spring

This year it took the queen's child. Lead a knight, a black mage and a white mage through a hand-built 3D overworld — forests, seas, mountains and a floating citadel — to break the thousand-year mist.

Classic turn-based command battles meet roguelike runs: every victory feeds a draft of relics and level-up cards that reshape your party. Four acts, one ending, and a New Game+ that keeps your heroes and raises the stakes.

A world that lives without you

  • Day-night cycle — the sun crosses the sky, dusk paints the horizon, stars come out at night.
  • Weather — rain sweeps the forest; the volcano rains embers; the void citadel breathes violet dust.
  • Region moods — light and color shift as you walk from shore to deep woods to mountain passes.
  • Chocobo riding doubles your speed; an airship unlocks in the third act and crosses the sea in one tap.

Fights that feel like FF

  • Turn forecast strip, limit-break cut-ins, hit shake and boss vignettes.
  • Enemies stand on a stone arena; your party stands opposite.
  • Every victory drafts relics — build a different engine every run.
