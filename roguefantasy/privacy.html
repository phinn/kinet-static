Privacy Policy

RogueFantasy for iOS · Last updated: November 2026

Short version

RogueFantasy collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no network access. Your saves live on your device and never leave it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Save data — party, gold, relics, world progress and settings, stored locally (UserDefaults and app-local JSON files).
  • Settings — language, sound toggles and the auto-battle preference, stored locally.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it permanently.

What the app does not do

  • No data collection of any kind (the App Store privacy label lists none).
  • No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no ads, no tracking domains.
  • No network requests at runtime — the game is fully offline.
  • No third-party content: all art, audio and text are original.

Children

The game contains fantasy violence (turn-based battles against monsters) with no blood, gore or realistic content. It collects no data, so no data of children is processed.
