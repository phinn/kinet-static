2 Frequently Asked

  • How do I move? Drag the left joystick to walk; tap anywhere on the ground for auto-pathing. A wooden arch marks each town gate — walk through it to enter.
  • How do battles work? The forecast strip shows turn order. Pick 攻击 (attack), 技能 (skills), 道具 (items) or 防御 (defend) from the command row. Every victory opens a draft: pick a relic or a level-up card.
  • Where is the airship? It unlocks during the third act — after the second act's finale you will get it with a toast. Once unlocked, tap the ground to fly anywhere; tap water or land again to descend.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. No account, no cloud, no network requests — uninstalling clears everything.
  • Is the game offline? Completely. RogueFantasy makes no network requests.
  • How do I change language? Settings ⚙️ → language. English, 简体中文, Deutsch and 日本語 are built in.

3 Troubleshooting

  • The screen looks dark at night. The world runs a 4-minute day-night cycle. Watch the sky — dawn comes around the corner.
  • It started raining. That is weather, not a glitch: 70 seconds of clear sky, then 30 seconds of rain. Light dims a little while it pours.
  • I cannot beat a boss. Grind a few road encounters for level drafts, buy gear in town (smith upgrades stack per visit), and use 防御 to wait out boss enrage turns.
  • My run ended — is it over? No. New Game+ carries your heroes' levels, skills and relics into a harder loop.
