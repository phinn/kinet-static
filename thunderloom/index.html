FAQ

How do I start a storm? / 怎么开始一场雷暴?

Click the Thunderloom icon in the menu bar and pick a storm mood. Lightning timing, flash color and thunder depth evolve continuously — press Reroll for a brand-new sky.

Is the thunder a recording? / 雷声是录音吗?

No. All audio is synthesized locally on your Mac from the same simulation that draws the lightning — flash and rumble always match.

Does it use the network? / 会联网吗?

Never. No streaming, no account, no tracking. Everything is generated on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里?

Entirely on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes all data.
