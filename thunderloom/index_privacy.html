Thunderloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Thunderloom is a menu bar application that generates lightning animations and synthesizes thunder-rain sounds locally on your Mac. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

Thunderloom does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All lightning rendering and audio synthesis run entirely on your device. Session statistics (duration, streaks) are stored in a local sandboxed database and never leave your Mac.

Third-Party Services

Thunderloom uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
