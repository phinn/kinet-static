SnapTile Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

SnapTile is a menu bar window tiling utility for macOS. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

SnapTile does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All window management runs entirely on your device using the macOS accessibility APIs.

Accessibility Permission

SnapTile requests the macOS Accessibility permission in order to read window positions and resize windows. This data is processed locally on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

SnapTile uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
