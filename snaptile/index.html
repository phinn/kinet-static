FAQ

How do I snap a window? / 怎么吸附窗口?

Focus the window and press the global hotkey, or click the SnapTile menu bar icon and pick a layout. Default: ⌃⌥← / ⌃⌥→ for halves.

Nothing happens when I press the hotkey / 热键没反应?

Grant SnapTile Accessibility permission: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → enable SnapTile, then try again.

Does it work with multiple displays? / 支持多显示器吗?

Yes. SnapTile snaps to the display the window is currently on.

Where is my data stored? / 数据存在哪里?

Entirely on your Mac. No account, no network, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
