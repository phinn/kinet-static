2 Frequently Asked

  • How do I play? Tap a tile to dig. Hold a tile to plant a flag. Clear every safe tile on a floor to open the card shop, then pick one of three cards to descend deeper.
  • Why did I lose my streak? Streaks advance when you clear a floor in Daily Challenge mode. Finishing a run on a mine does not break it; missing a day does.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so uninstalling the app clears everything. That also means your save cannot be restored on a new device.
  • Is the game offline? Completely. Minebreaker makes no network requests at all, so it works in airplane mode.
  • How do I turn the sound off? Tap the ♪ button in the top-right of the game board.

3 Troubleshooting

  • The board looks squashed. Minebreaker is designed for portrait iPhone. Rotate back to portrait and reopen the app if the layout looks off.
  • Nothing happens when I tap. If a floor-clear or cave-in panel is open, tap it to dismiss it first, then dig.
  • Daily Challenge looks different from my friend's. That is intended: every player gets the same board for a given day, so runs are directly comparable.
