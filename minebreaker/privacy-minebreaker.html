Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-26 · Minebreaker for iOS

Short answer

Minebreaker collects nothing, tracks nothing, and sends nothing.

Minebreaker is an entirely offline, single-player game. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no network access of any kind. We do not receive your personal information because the app never transmits any.

1 What the App Stores

Your game progress is saved only on this device, using the iOS app sandbox (NSUserDefaults). This data is never uploaded, synced to a server, or shared with us. It includes things like:

  • Sound on/off setting
  • Daily Challenge completion and streak counters
  • Any other local preference you set inside the app

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of it.

2 No Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or trade any personal information — no name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, advertising identifier, IP address, location, contacts, photos, health data, or usage analytics.

The App Store privacy label for Minebreaker is therefore set to Data Not Collected.

3 No Tracking

Minebreaker does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

4 No Internet Access

Minebreaker has no networking code and makes no outbound connections. There is no account to create, no server to reach, and nothing that can fail because you are offline. You can play the full game in airplane mode.

5 Children

Minebreaker is suitable for all ages and contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, no user-generated content, and no data collection of any kind. It is rated 4+ in the App Store.

6 Your Control

Because all data lives on your device, you have full control by design: uninstalling Minebreaker deletes everything, and no support ticket or account-deletion request is needed — there is nothing on our side to delete, because we hold nothing.

7 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

8 Contact

Questions about this policy? Email phinn@outlook.com. We typically reply within a few days.
