Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-23 · Applies to KinetAios iOS apps

The one-line version

We collect nothing. Your data never leaves your device.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The apps work fully offline — there is no server to send anything to.

1 Data We Collect

None. Our apps do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Identity data — no name, email, phone number, or account of any kind
  • Location data — no GPS, no region detection
  • Usage data — no analytics, no session tracking, no crash reporting services
  • Advertising identifiers — no IDFA, no ad SDKs, no cross-app tracking
  • Contacts, photos, health, or financial data — never requested

2 Data Stored on Your Device

Apps save your progress locally on your device only, using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults / on-device files). For example, CubeLike stores your current level, stars, and settings so you can resume where you left off.

This data is readable only by the app, synced nowhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

3 Third-Party Services

None. No third-party SDKs, trackers, ad networks, or analytics frameworks are embedded. App distribution and payment are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; Apple's own privacy policy applies to store transactions.

4 Network Access

The apps do not require internet access and make no network requests during normal use. If a future version adds an optional online feature, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the feature will always be opt-in.

5 Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data at all, the apps are equally safe for children and adults. No age-gated content, no chat, no user-generated content sharing.

6 Your Rights & Control

You have full control by design: all data lives in your hands, on your device. Deleting the app deletes everything. No support ticket or account deletion flow is needed because there is nothing to delete on our side — we hold nothing.

7 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

8 Contact

Questions about this policy? Email phinn@outlook.com. We typically reply within a few days.
