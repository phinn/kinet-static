⛏ Minebreaker

Minesweeper, broken open. Dig the vein, dodge the blast, build your deck.
iOS 17+ · Free · No ads · No tracking

The classic, rebuilt as a dig site

Every minefield is a mine vein. Clear the floor, pocket the ore, then choose one of three cards to push deeper — shields, scrying, auto-flagging, doubling gambles. The further down you go, the richer and deadlier the vein.

Why it sticks

  • Runs, not puzzles. Chained reveals feel like mining; death sends you back to Floor 1 with more knowledge.
  • 8-card pool. Build a different engine every run — tank it with Defuse, or sprint with the Gambler.
  • Minutes to learn. Tap to dig, long-press to flag. Everything else is the minefield you already know.
  • Offline forever. No account, no network, no interruptions. Your save lives on your device.
