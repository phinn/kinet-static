KinetEmber Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

KinetEmber is a menu bar application that generates campfire animations and fire crackle sounds locally on your Mac. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

KinetEmber does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All flame rendering and audio synthesis run entirely on your device.

Third-Party Services

KinetEmber uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact
