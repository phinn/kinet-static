Sonalyx

Live sound level & spectrum — precise, private, offline.

Instrument-grade measurement

  • dB(A) / dB(C) / dB(Z) — IEC 61672 frequency weightings, computed live
  • Leq energy average, plus MIN / MAX hold
  • 48-band spectrum, 31.5 Hz – 16 kHz, log-scaled
  • FFT via Apple Accelerate — 2048-point, Hann window, 48 kHz

Absolute privacy

Sonalyx works 100% offline. Audio is analyzed in real time on your device and never recorded, stored, or transmitted. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking — read the privacy policy.

Designed like an instrument

Dark, glanceable, one-handed. The reading is color-coded from calm green to alarm red, with a live spectrum at your fingertips.
