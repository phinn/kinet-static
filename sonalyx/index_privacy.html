Sonalyx Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

Sonalyx is an offline sound level meter for iPhone and iPad. We collect no data whatsoever.

Microphone Access

Sonalyx uses the microphone solely to measure ambient sound levels in real time. All audio analysis is performed on-device using Apple's Accelerate framework. No audio is recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared. The moment you stop measurement, no audio data exists anywhere.

Data Collection

Sonalyx does not collect, transmit, store (off-device), or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Data Storage

Your calibration offset and preferences are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

Sonalyx uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Sonalyx contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.
