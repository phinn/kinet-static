TillDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

TillDay is a menu bar event countdown app for macOS. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

TillDay does not collect, transmit, store (off-device), or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All events, preferences, and generated visuals live entirely on your Mac inside the app's sandbox container.

Data Storage

Your events are stored locally on your device using the standard macOS user defaults system. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

TillDay uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

TillDay contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
