FAQ

How do I add an event?怎么添加事件?イベントの追加方法は?

Click the TillDay icon in the menu bar, then click the “+” button.

Does it support lunar-calendar repeats?支持农历重复吗?旧暦の繰り返しに対応していますか?

Yes — birthdays and holidays can repeat by the lunar calendar, correctly handling leap months.

Where is my data stored?数据存在哪里?
