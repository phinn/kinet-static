Privacy Policy

Involect · Updated October 2026 · 中文在下方

Short version

Involect does not collect anything. No account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Invoices, quotes, and client details you enter never leave your device.

Permissions

The app uses no network permission and requests no personal data. PDF export hands the document straight to the iOS share sheet — you choose where it goes.

Storage & deletion

All records live in the app's local database. Delete the app and every record is permanently erased. There are no server copies.

Changes

If a future version ever adds networking, this page and the App Store listing will say so first. There is currently no such plan.
