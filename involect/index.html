FAQ

  1. Where is my data stored?
    On your device only. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account to create.
  2. Can I use one purchase on iPhone and Mac?
    Yes — the iOS and macOS versions are separate apps, each free to download.
  3. How do I change tax on a single line?
    Edit the line item and set its rate; totals recalculate instantly.
  4. Lost my records after deleting the app?
    Local data is removed with the app. Export PDFs before uninstalling if you need copies.
