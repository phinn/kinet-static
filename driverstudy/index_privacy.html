KinetDriver Study — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

KinetDriver Study is a driving theory test study app covering 45 countries. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

KinetDriver Study does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind. All question banks, practice history, mock exam results, and settings live entirely on your iPhone inside the app's sandbox.

Data Storage

Your progress, wrong-answer notebook, and preferences are stored locally on your device. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

KinetDriver Study uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The app contains no telemetry and requires no account.

Children

KinetDriver Study contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
