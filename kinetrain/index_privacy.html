KinetRain Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

KinetRain is a menu bar application that generates rain sounds and rain animations locally on your Mac. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

KinetRain does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests. All audio synthesis and animations run entirely on your device.

Third-Party Services

KinetRain uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
