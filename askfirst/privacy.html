AskFirst Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: 2026-10-14

What AskFirst does / AskFirst 是什么

AskFirst is a companion app that shows your coding agent's pending tool calls (shell commands, file edits, etc.) on your iPhone, and lets you approve or deny them. / AskFirst 把编码 Agent 待执行的工具调用(shell 命令、文件编辑等)展示在 iPhone 上,由你决定放行或拒绝。

Data collection / 数据收集

AskFirst collects no analytics, no tracking, no ads. / AskFirst 不收集任何分析数据、不追踪、无广告。

Approval requests may contain tool-call metadata (command text, file paths, timestamps) generated by your own development tools on your own devices. This data is stored locally on your devices and, if you enable iCloud sync, in your personal iCloud account (CloudKit). We never see it. / 审批请求包含你自己设备上开发工具产生的工具调用元数据(命令文本、文件路径、时间戳)。这些数据仅存储在你的设备和你的个人 iCloud(CloudKit)中,我们无法查看。

Data storage / 数据存储

  • On-device: decisions, history and settings are stored in the app sandbox. / 决定、历史与设置存储在应用沙盒内。
  • iCloud (optional): pairing and approval sync via Apple CloudKit under your Apple ID. / 可选的 iCloud 同步通过你的 Apple ID 下的 CloudKit 完成。
  • No third-party servers. / 不经过任何第三方服务器。
