Clasp

Privacy Policy · Effective 2026-10-09

Clasp collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no network.

Your focus sessions never leave your device.

What the app stores

  • Session history (start time, planned length, interruptions, outcome) — saved in the app's own storage on your device only.
  • Your chosen session length — a single number in the system defaults store.
  • Whether you've seen the introduction — one on/off flag.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server, no sync, no backup we control.

Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications — used only to tell you when a timed session completes, in case the lid is closed and the screen isn't visible. Optional; the app works without it.

Clasp does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo, or health access.

What we don't do

  • No data collection of any kind
  • No advertising or trackers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No account or sign-in
  • No network connections at runtime

Children

Clasp contains no mature content, no chat, and no data collection, and is safe for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this URL with a new effective date.
