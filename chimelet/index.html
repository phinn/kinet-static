Chimelet

A bell for every hour, beautifully marked.

Chimelet lives quietly in your menu bar and strikes the hour with handcrafted bell voices — synthesized live on your Mac, never streamed. Six voices from a Westminster clock to a temple gong, each tuned like an instrument, not an alarm.

Six voices

Westminster

The classic four-note hour count.

Harbor Bell

Deep and slow, like a foghorn in reverse.

Temple Gong

A single, resonant, lingering tone.

Wind Chime

Light, random, and a little whimsical.

Music Box

Tiny notes, tinkling and gentle.

Orpheus

A soft sine sweep, almost singing.

Tuned to your day

Hourly strikes that count the hour — three chimes at three, twelve at noon. A lighter sub-chime on the half-hour. Night quiet hours you set once and forget. Wake-from-sleep resync that never replays the hours you slept through.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account. No network. No analytics. The bell sounds are synthesized on-device. Your schedule, voice choice, and quiet hours live in the app's sandbox container and nowhere else.

One click, right where you are

Chimelet lives in your menu bar. Click the icon to preview any voice, change the schedule, or adjust the volume. Nothing else to learn.
