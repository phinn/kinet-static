Dayoku Ginkgo Calendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

Dayoku Ginkgo Calendar (银杏台历) is a desk calendar that exercises your brain. We collect no data whatsoever.

Data Collection

Dayoku does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind. All puzzles, progress, streaks, and settings live entirely on your iPhone inside the app's sandbox.

Data Storage

Your streak, completed pages, leaf collection, and preferences are stored locally on your device. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Third-Party Services

Dayoku uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. The app contains no telemetry and requires no account.

Children

Dayoku contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
