FAQ

What does "First Aid" actually do?

It samples system-wide CPU, memory, disk, network, battery and chip temperature every two seconds, then names the most likely cause of the slowdown: a busy app, memory pressure, heat, low disk space or low battery.

Why can't the App Store version quit a process?

macOS sandboxing — required for App Store distribution — does not allow one app to terminate another. KinetMacPulse therefore diagnoses and points you to the culprit; you can quit that app yourself, or use Activity Monitor. The direct-download edition of KinetMacPulse can quit the process for you.

Does it collect any data?

No. Everything runs locally. There is no account, no analytics, no network access. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does the temperature reading differ from other tools?

KinetMacPulse reads the Apple Silicon die sensors directly and shows the hottest sensor ("Chip max"), which is normally a few degrees above what generic tools report.

How do I quit the app?

Click the menu bar readout, then the power button at the bottom of the panel — or right-click the menu bar icon and choose Quit.
