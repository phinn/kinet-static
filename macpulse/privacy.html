KinetMacPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

KinetMacPulse collects no personal data, contains no analytics, and makes no network requests. Everything it measures stays on your Mac.

What the app reads

  • System-wide CPU, memory, disk and network counters (provided by macOS).
  • Battery and power state (provided by macOS).
  • Chip temperature sensors on Apple Silicon (read locally through the IOHID sensor service).
  • The list of running applications and their CPU/memory usage (provided by macOS).

These values are used only to render the panel and the detail windows. They are never stored on disk, except for your own preferences such as the heat-alert threshold and menu bar options, which are kept in macOS UserDefaults on your Mac.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or identifier of any kind.
  • No telemetry, crash reporting, or usage analytics.
  • No network connections — the app does not include networking code.
  • No access to your documents, photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Notifications

If you enable heat alerts, KinetMacPulse may post a local notification when the chip exceeds your threshold. Notifications are generated on-device; no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the panel or in System Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
