Support

How it works, and what to do when it doesn't.

Frequently asked

  • How do I add photos? Open Settings (the gear icon), tap "Choose photos", pick from your library. Photos are copied into the app, so no library permission is needed.
  • My photos disappeared after restarting. They are saved on disk and reload on launch — if the photo page looks empty, switch tabs or wait a second while they decode.
  • Can it show lunar dates? Yes — Chinese locale shows lunar month/day, solar terms and traditional festivals automatically.
  • Does it work without the dual-screen device? Yes. On a regular iPhone or iPad it becomes two tabs: calendar and photos.
