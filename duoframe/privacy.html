Privacy Policy

Short version: nothing leaves your device.

Data collection

DuoFrame collects no data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network permission. It does not create an account and does not know who you are.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's own private storage on your device. The app never reads your photo library in the background, never uploads photos, and never shares them. Deleting the app deletes the copies permanently.

Data storage

Your calendar preferences and photo choices are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

None. DuoFrame makes no network requests.
