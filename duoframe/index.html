DuoFrame

A desk calendar that flips — with your photos on the other page.

What it does

DuoFrame turns a device into a paper-style desk calendar: an extra-large day page with lunar dates and solar terms, and a photo page that slowly cycles your own pictures with a gentle Ken Burns drift. On dual-screen devices it stands like a real tent calendar — the calendar faces you, the photos face the person across the table.

Highlights

  • Extra-large type up to 120 pt — readable across the room
  • Lunar calendar, solar terms and festivals for Chinese users
  • Photo slideshow from your own picks; nothing is uploaded
  • Interval, shuffle and font-size controls
  • Covered lid? The outer screen becomes a big clock

Privacy in one line

No accounts, no analytics, no network. Photos you pick are copied into the app's own storage on your device and stay there. Read the full policy →
